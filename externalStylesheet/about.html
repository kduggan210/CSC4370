About The Website

Kyele Duggan

I am an avid believer that watching a movie in a cinema surrounded by dozens of other people is an experience that cannot be easily replicated anywhere else. This is of course true for new releases but especially true for rereleases. The feeling of reliving a cherished experience in this atmosphere, or getting to experience these events for the first time is one I hope anyone can find with maximum ease.

In pursuit of making this aforementioned experience easier to find, I've decided to build a website dedicated to tracking theatrical rereleases and special editions that would fly under the radar of most users. Simply going to the main page of any exhibitor's website, you would understandably be met with advertisements for the hottest, new movies in theaters that week, or a big new release coming up on the weekend. Without going out of their way to look for it, a user may have no idea that one of their favorite movies is showing on the big screen for the first time in years right then! Alternatively, a movie they have been meaning to getting around to watching could be rereleasing in a matter of weeks, making for the perfect occasion to get that first watch in. To aid fans in making these showings simple to find, my website provides information for all upcoming and ongoing rereleases and special edition showings in theaters, tailored to the location and specified interests of the user.
